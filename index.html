Connectors

Pull data from Google Analytics 4, Search Console, Google Ads and more — one connector at a time, no bloat.

Schedules

Set a cron schedule once. Vyom refreshes your data automatically, every day or hour.

Destinations

CSV, JSON, Google Sheets. Your data lands where you actually use it.

Open API

Every dataset is queryable over a simple REST API — feed dashboards, Claude, or anything else.
