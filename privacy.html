Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Vyom is a self-hosted, open marketing data pipeline. This page describes how the Vyom application handles data.

Data we access

When you connect a data source (for example Google Analytics 4 or Google Search Console), Vyom requests read-only access to your reporting data via Google's OAuth APIs. Vyom stores only: your OAuth refresh token, your connector configuration, and the data pulled by jobs you create.

Where data is stored

All data — credentials, configuration and pulled datasets — is stored locally on the machine where Vyom runs. Vyom's developers do not receive, transmit or store copies of your data on any external server.

How data is used

Data pulled by Vyom is used solely to fulfill the jobs you configure: writing datasets to files, spreadsheets, or serving them through Vyom's local API.

Revoking access

You can revoke Vyom's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete local data by removing the Vyom folder from your machine.
